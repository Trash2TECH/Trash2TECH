What can we do as students?

1. College E-Waste Collection Drive
We can keep one box in our college canteen or lab. Tell all students to bring old chargers, earphones, batteries. At end of week, we can give it to Eco Friend Industries in Pawane. They are near, only 5-6 km from colleges in Navi Mumbai.

2. Visit Real Centre - Field Visit
We can plan a small field visit to recycling centre. For example, Eco Friend Industries at A-205, TTC Pawane is open 24 hours. We can call them on 022-27689020 and ask for 1 hour visit. It will be useful for our project report and we can see how they recycle.

3. Awareness in Our Building / Society
In our own building, we can make one poster - "Don't throw e-waste in dustbin, give it here". Keep a small carton in society office. Many families have old remotes, wires. They will give if we ask.

4. Simple DIY Project
From old e-waste we can make something useful. Like old CPU can be made into book stand, old keyboard keys can be used for art project. We can show this in our college tech fest.

5. Internship / Volunteering
Some centres in Navi Mumbai like Antony Revive in Pawane take students for internship for 2-3 days. We can mail them and learn how e-waste business works. We can add this in our resume for 3rd sem.

Our Plan for This Month:
Week 1 - Make poster and collection box
Week 2 - Collect from our class
Week 3 - Visit Pawane centre
Week 4 - Submit collected e-waste and take photos for report
