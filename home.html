Welcome!

Trash2Tech is for Navi Mumbai students to convert e-waste into money.

We have 1000+ devices collected, 400kg recycled.

Why we should think about e-waste?

We buy new phones every 1-2 years, but where does old phone go? Most of us keep it in drawer or throw in dustbin. In Navi Mumbai, this e-waste goes to Turbhe dumping ground. Inside old phone there is battery and chemicals which mix with soil and water. We drink same water, so we are harming our own life.

One house has 2-3 old chargers and 1 old mobile. Navi Mumbai has lakhs of houses, so total e-waste is very big. We say we care about environment but we don't know where to give old gadgets. That is why we made Trash2Tech - to show nearby centres like Eco Friend in Pawane MIDC. If our college with 2000 students gives 1 charger each, we can collect 50kg e-waste easily. Small step but useful for our city.
