How to Give E-Waste

We only give information. We don't collect e-waste.

Step 1 - Check Type
Small - charger, earphone, mouse
Medium - mobile, power bank
Big - laptop, printer, TV

Step 2 - Pack
Remove SIM card, delete data, put in one bag.

Step 3 - Pickup or Drop
Pickup: If more than 10kg from college, call centre for free pickup
Drop: If 1-2 items, you have to go to centre in Pawane

Call Script:
College se bol rahe hain, e-waste hai, pickup hoga kya?

Note: Pickup is done by centre, not by us.
